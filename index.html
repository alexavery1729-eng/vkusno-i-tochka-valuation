Ownership structure

Method, in short

  • Perimeter limited to confirmed ownership links. Two companies were dropped because joint stock company shareholder registers are not public in Russia and the link could not be verified.
  • Earnings normalised on 2023–2025. The 2022 loss of ₽11.3bn reflects the handover of 850 restaurants, not trading, and is excluded from the base.
  • Double counting removed: holding companies whose only assets are shares in the operating companies are not added to the group total.
  • Discount rate of 22% derived from the 10-year OFZ yield of 16.2% plus equity, size and illiquidity premia, less long-term growth.
  • No EV/EBITDA: depreciation is not disclosed in Russian statutory forms.
Sources: EGRUL (Federal Tax Service) and GIR BO (bo.nalog.gov.ru). Russian statutory accounts, not IFRS. Data as of 21 October 2026. This is a desktop valuation from public sources — not an appraisal report under FSO or RICS standards, and not investment advice.
